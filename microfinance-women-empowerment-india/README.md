# Microfinance & Women's Empowerment in India
## Research Question
How do microfinance initiatives and Self-Help Groups contribute to women's economic development and empowerment in India?
## Area
Development Economics | Financial Inclusion | Gender & Economics
## Approach
The paper examines existing research on microfinance and Self-Help Groups, focusing on their effects on women's income, savings, entrepreneurship, financial literacy, household decision-making and social participation.
## Key Themes
- Financial inclusion
- Women's economic empowerment
- Self-employment and entrepreneurship
- Financial literacy
- Household bargaining power
- Social capital
## Limitations
The paper also considers limitations of microfinance, including its concentration in self-employment and informal-sector activities, implementation challenges and difficulties reaching remote rural communities.

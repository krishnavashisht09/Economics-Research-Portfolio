## Research Projects
### 1. Mid-Day Meal Scheme & Primary Education in Tamil Nadu
**Area:** Econometrics | Public Policy
An econometric analysis examining the relationship between Mid-Day Meal Scheme budget allocation and elementary gross enrolment in Tamil Nadu using 2003–2025 time-series data and an OLS log-log model.
[View Project](./mid-day-meal-tamil-nadu)
### 2. Microfinance & Women's Empowerment in India
**Area:** Development Economics
An analysis of the role of microfinance and Self-Help Groups in advancing women's economic empowerment, with a focus on income, financial autonomy, entrepreneurship and household decision-making.
